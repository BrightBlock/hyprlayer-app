# Project pipeline board

Opening a project with no workstream selected shows its pipeline. With no workstreams, it is a blank state with one call to action. Otherwise a `Pipeline view` switch picks one of three views. `Board` has five columns: Planning, Implementing, Waiting, Review, Merged. `Triage` groups workstreams by live activity, and `Table` is one row each. A workstream's column follows live activity first, then its PR, then its stage. Clicking a card opens the workstream. The header's `h1` is itself the project picker, and the header has `New worktree` (or `New session` in a folder project). A git project with the Lead enabled also gets a `Lead` chip in the header and a `Lead Engineer` dock under the board; a folder project gets neither. <!-- src: src/features/board/ProjectPipeline.tsx:188 --> <!-- src: src/features/board/ProjectPipeline.tsx:127 -->

## Sub-features

- `board-blank` shows the empty project's blank state (`first session.` / `first branch.`) and its `New session`/`New worktree` call to action.
- `board-columns` places each workstream in a column by stage, and the column header shows its count.
- `board-views` switches between `Triage`, `Board` and `Table`, and the choice persists.
- `board-open` opens a workstream by clicking its card.
- `board-project-picker` switches projects from the header picker.
- `board-new` opens New workstream from the header button.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click a project row in the sidebar (no workstream selected), or the project name in a workstream's header crumb. <!-- src: src/routes/Root.tsx:1359 --> <!-- src: src/routes/Root.tsx:922 -->

## Gotchas

- The view persists per Vite origin, so a reused port can open in Table or Triage. Click `Board` before asserting columns. <!-- src: src/features/board/pipelineViewStore.ts:18 -->
- Project paths are canonicalised (`/var/folders/…` becomes `/private/var/folders/…`), so take `projectPath` from `get_bootstrap().projects[].path`, not the `$TMPDIR` spelling. This applies to `[data-project-path=…]` selectors and every IPC arg. <!-- src: crates/hyprlayer-core/src/runtime/projects.rs:745 --> <!-- src: src/routes/Root.tsx:1307 -->
- `set_workstream_stage` only moves forward along research → … → revise. `general` has no rank, so setting it is a no-op, and any ranked stage beats it. <!-- src: crates/hyprlayer-core/src/pipeline.rs:21 --> <!-- src: crates/hyprlayer-core/src/store/workstreams.rs:358 -->
- A `general` workstream with uncommitted or ahead-of-base changes lands in Implementing, not Planning. Untracked files don't count. <!-- src: src/features/board/pipeline.ts:30 -->
- `Waiting` needs a live `needs-you` session, and `Merged` needs an observed merged PR or a same-day merged archive. Neither can be seeded without a real agent or PR. <!-- src: src/features/board/pipeline.ts:35 --> <!-- src: src/features/board/pipeline.ts:69 -->
- In a folder project, cards have no context menu, so `Attach PR…` and `Run until met…` are git-only. <!-- src: src/routes/ProjectPage.tsx:1342 -->
- On a git project with the Lead on (the default), the header shows a `Lead` chip, reading `Lead · driving N of M` once the Lead drives workstreams, and the board ends in a `Lead Engineer` dock: `No replies yet` until the Lead answers, and an input `[aria-label="Message the Lead Engineer"]` whose Send starts a Lead turn. The `review_lifecycle` sandbox shows the chip and dock for free; don't send. Once the Lead holds a session, the main workstream leaves the board and a waiting Lead gets a `Lead Engineer` card at the top of Waiting. <!-- src: src/features/lead/LeadChip.tsx:18 --> <!-- src: src/features/lead/LeadDock.tsx:295 --> <!-- src: src/features/board/PipelineBoard.tsx:69 -->
- A goal running on a workstream adds a `[data-testid="board-goal-badge"]` reading `goal N/M`. <!-- src: src/features/board/PipelineCard.tsx:122 -->
